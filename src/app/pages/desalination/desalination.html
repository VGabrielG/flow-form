1. Energy & Carbon Costs

10/10 Priority
The Problem

Desalination is incredibly energy-intensive. As countries implement carbon taxes (such as in Chile and the EU), the absolute top priority is reducing the energy required to push water through membranes and powering the process with renewable sources (solar, wind, storage).

Flowform Contribution: NO

While passive, it does not reduce the fundamental energy needed for reverse osmosis.

2. PFAS & Emerging Toxins

9/10 Priority
The Problem

Regulatory standards in 2026 are tightening dramatically around "forever chemicals" (PFAS) and microplastics. Existing plants are being forced to add entirely new, extremely expensive treatment stages just to legally comply and remain operational.

Flowform Contribution: NO

This requires chemical or molecular-level filtration that Flowform does not provide.

1. Brine Environmental Impact

8/10 Priority
The Problem

Brine discharge is often concentrated, dense, high-temperature, and critically low in oxygen. It sinks to the seafloor, creating localized "dead zones" that devastate marine life. Regulators are increasingly using brine impact as the primary reason to deny or delay plant permits.

Flowform Contribution: YES (HIGH VALUE)

Flowform's capability for passive aeration directly targets this. By pre-treating brine with oxygen before discharge, it lowers the environmental risk and simplifies the regulatory approval process.

2. Membrane Biofouling

7/10 Priority
The Problem

Bacteria constantly build layers of biofilm on the membranes. This forces operators to increase pumping pressure (wasting energy) and perform frequent, harsh chemical cleanings that shorten the membrane lifespan. It is a constant, expensive $Opex$ drain.

Flowform Contribution: YES (HIGH VALUE)

The unique, rhythmic movement of the water disrupts the initial settling of bacteria. If used in pre-treatment, it can reduce the need for biocides (chlorine) and extend the life of the primary reverse osmosis membranes.
